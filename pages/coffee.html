NEPAL'S COFFEE

Grown With Character.

Nepal's hills provide a unique environment for growing coffee, with high elevations, cool temperatures, and rich natural surroundings.

At CHAUTARI, we celebrate the character of Nepali coffee by giving every cup the attention it deserves.

THE JOURNEY

From Bean To Cup.

Every cup is part of a longer journey.

01

Grown

Coffee begins in the hills, where carefully cultivated plants grow among Nepal's natural landscapes.

02

Selected

Carefully selected beans are prepared to preserve their natural qualities and distinctive character.

03

Roasted

Roasting brings out the aroma, flavour, and personality hidden inside every bean.

04

Brewed

Finally, the beans become a carefully prepared cup made to be enjoyed slowly.
