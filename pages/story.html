THE IDEA BEHIND CHAUTARI

More Than A Café.

A place inspired by the simple Nepali tradition of stopping, resting, and sharing time with others.

WHERE IT BEGAN

A Place To Pause.

In Nepal, a chautari has traditionally been a simple resting place along a journey. Beneath the shade of a tree, people would stop, rest, meet others, and share stories.

That simple idea became the inspiration for CHAUTARI — a modern café designed around the feeling of slowing down and being present.

A PLACE TO GATHER

Come For The Coffee.

Stay for the conversations.

CHAUTARI is designed as a space where people can meet without rushing. Whether it is a quiet morning alone, an afternoon with friends, or a long conversation over coffee, there is always room for another story.
